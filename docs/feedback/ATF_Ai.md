# Research and qualification of players

## Overview

Whist policy manager holds these research and qualification criteria. The rules already implemented are described in [modelul deciziei](../ai/ATF_DecisionModel.md), promotion follows [protocolul offline](../learning/README.md).

## Levels and qualification limits

| Priority | Subiect | Closure status and criterion |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Book incidence at 2–8 cards | The overall weighting of each opponent's behavior remains experimental. Activation requires identified pattern, legal support, regularization and separate progress on new lots. The active model of a card has separate domain. |
| P1 | Probability Interpretation | The feasible sampling and simulation average does not certify uniformity or calibration. Please state the distribution, budget and uncertainty before using the estimation as probability. |
| P1 | Cost on target environment and strategic progress | [Calificarea JVM](../ai/ATF_CostQualification.md) holds components, budgets and measurement in positions generated by 2–8 cards. Latency and consumption on the device require identified Android profile; strategic progress requires separate evaluation on the role, opponent and place, with frozen sources. None of them are deduced from the cost of JVM. |
| P2 | Reproduction of independence between contexts | Hard uses score differences in the initialization of the estimate, according to [contract of decision](../ai/ATF_DecisionModel.md#reproducibility-and-limits). The independent player keeps absolute scores in his own seed; the extension of the same invariant requires his own components and independent policy verification. |

## Qualification contracts

The above open works are verified by responsible contracts; a change of documentation does not qualify the policy.

| Criteriu obligatoriu | contract and border |
|---|---|
| Information and model | [Behavior inference](../ai/ATF_RulesConfiguration.md#behavior-inference): reconstruction without duplication, without future, possible undivided cards and support without observations, constant or focused. Individual factors do not certify the common distribution of opponents. |
| Independence of mechanisms | [Modelul deciziei](../ai/ATF_DecisionModel.md): separate legal restriction, voluntary plausible, continuation and utility; a sample of the helper remains limited to that API. |
| Guarantee and reserve | [Componentele AI](../testing/ATF_AiComponents.md): allowed observation, independent quantifiers and explicit behaviour outside the area or budget exhausted. |
| Promotion and data | [Protocolul offline](../learning/README.md#the-improvement-cycle): freezing, separation of the valuation adjustment and all the criteria declared; results and decision kept externally. |
